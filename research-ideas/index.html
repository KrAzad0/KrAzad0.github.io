Flagship research project

Hydrogen in a torus

How does toroidal confinement change the energy spectrum and attosecond dynamics of a hydrogenic electron?

Project on GitHub

A proton near the torus centreline supplies the Coulomb potential. The electron obeys hard-wall Dirichlet conditions. A laser field with a Gaussian pulse envelope drives its time-dependent Schrödinger dynamics.

Ω = { (ρ, φ, z) : (ρ − R)² + z² < a² },   R > a > 0

How do the bound-state energies, probability density, and probability current vary with the two torus radii? Candidate extensions include tunnelling between localized regions, harmonic response, spin dynamics, and geometric phases.

  1. Compute field-free eigenstates and compare converged energies across geometries.
  2. Propagate a chosen initial state through the pulse in MATLAB and check norm conservation and time-step convergence.
  3. Compare populations, density, and probability current across radii and pulse parameters.

Quantum dynamicsToroidal hydrogen and attosecond pulses

How does the geometry of a toroidal hard wall affect hydrogenic bound states and driven dynamics?

Starting point. Benchmark field-free eigenstates before adding the laser pulse.

Quantum algorithmsQuantum algorithms for physical differential equations

Can a quantum or hybrid method reproduce a small, explicitly defined TDSE benchmark?

Starting point. Compare quantum Hamiltonian simulation with finite-difference, spectral, or Krylov methods using matched accuracy criteria.

Mathematical physicsPositive geometry and scattering amplitudes

Can twistor variables or positive geometry simplify a specific scattering-amplitude calculation?

Starting point. Reproduce a known amplituhedron example, then identify which ingredients would need to change for a specified QCD process.

Scientific AIStatistical physics of learning machines

Can a tractable learning model show changes in generalization as a control parameter varies?

Starting point. Choose a model and dataset family, measure learning curves and loss, and test whether apparent transitions persist with scale.

Quantum biologyElectron and proton tunnelling in molecules

How do molecular geometry, temperature and environmental coupling influence tunnelling in a model system?

Starting point. Compare a minimal molecular barrier model with an environment-coupled model.

Quantum devicesSpintronics and spin transport

How does spin–orbit coupling affect spin transport in a quantum dot or a nanoscale junction?

Starting point. Calculate spin-dependent transmission in a minimal Hamiltonian. Other directions include spin Hall response, skyrmions, bimerons, and Heusler interfaces.

Mathematical physicsRamanujan’s Lost Notebook and mathematical physics

Can a specific identity involving q-series, modular forms, or partitions be linked to a meaningful quantity in a physical model?

Starting point. Select one identity, check its known interpretations, and derive its connection to a model such as a partition function.

AstrophysicsNeutron-star mergers and scattering observables

Which scattering observable or density correlation can be calculated for a specified merger environment?

Starting point. Define the observable and test its sensitivity to a simplified density profile or nuclear matter equation of state.

Open quantum systemsThermalisation, decoherence, and quantum simulation

How well can a quantum simulation reproduce the relaxation and coherence decay of a small open system?

Starting point. Solve a Lindblad master equation for a few levels and compare populations and coherences across implementations.

Quantum chemistryVariational algorithms for molecular simulation

How do variational quantum and classical molecular energies compare across the bond lengths of H₂?

Starting point. Compare exact diagonalisation in a fixed basis, Hartree–Fock, and VQE under matched assumptions. The interactive H₂ simulator arose as an assistant-proposed extension.
