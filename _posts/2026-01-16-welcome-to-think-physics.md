## A New Journey Begins

Today marks the first day of my **Think Physics** daily journal – a space where I will share daily thoughts, research updates, and insights into the fascinating worlds of quantum mechanics, attosecond physics, and computational science.

## What This Blog Is About

This blog will serve as:
- **Daily reflections** on theoretical physics concepts
- **Research progress updates** on hydrogen atom dynamics and attosecond pulses
- **Problem-solving notes** from graduate-level coursework
- **Exploration of cutting-edge topics** like amplituhedrons and quantum computing
- **Learning log** for mathematics and physics fundamentals

## Current Research Interests

My primary focus areas include:

1. **Attosecond Physics** – Understanding ultrafast electron dynamics in atoms
2. **Quantum Mechanics** – Solving the Schrödinger equation for complex systems
3. **Computational Physics** – Simulating quantum systems using Python and MATLAB
4. **Quantum Field Theory** – Exploring the mathematical structures underlying particle physics
5. **Positive Geometry** – Investigating amplituhedrons and scattering amplitudes

## Why "Think.ai"?

The philosophy behind this name is simple: **think deeply, think systematically, think openly**. Like an AI system processing information layer by layer, I want to approach physics by breaking down complex problems into their fundamental components.
