# Think Physics – A Daily Physics Journal

Welcome to **Think Physics**, where I share daily insights, research notes, and reflections on physics, mathematics, and computational science.

## About This Blog

I'm **Kumar Azad**, a physics researcher from Mathura Pur, Bihar, India. I hold a BSc in Physics and am currently pursuing an MSc in Physics and Mathematics simultaneously. This blog serves as a comprehensive repository of my:

- **Daily research notes** on attosecond physics and quantum mechanics
- **Problem-solving sessions** from graduate-level coursework
- **Computational physics projects** and simulations
- **Learning reflections** on theoretical physics concepts
- **Academic journey** towards PhD applications

## Current Research Interests

🔬 **Attosecond Physics** – Ultrafast electron dynamics in atoms under intense laser fields

⚛️ **Quantum Mechanics** – Solving the Schrödinger equation for hydrogen-like atoms and complex systems

💻 **Computational Physics** – Numerical simulations using Python, MATLAB, and Mathematica

📐 **Quantum Field Theory** – Exploring mathematical structures in particle physics

✨ **Positive Geometry** – Investigating amplituhedrons and scattering amplitudes
