Selected Research

Mathematical Formulation from Ramanujan's Lost Notebook
2025 - Present | Independent Theoretical Work

Investigating identities and series from Ramanujan's Lost Notebook to develop rigorous integral transforms. The aim is to use these formulations to model and analyze physics problems in strong-interaction and field-theoretic settings.

Positive Geometry in Scattering Amplitudes
2025 | ICTS Research Program

Explored geometric formulations of scattering amplitudes with applications to QCD kinematics and twistor-space mapping. Focused on Scattering Dynamics and Amplituhedron Geometry.

Electron Dynamics in Attosecond Laser-Torus Systems
2024 | AISILS Research Program

Studied charged-particle dynamics under intense fields and toroidal confinement using numerical TDSE (Time-Dependent Schrödinger Equation) solvers.
