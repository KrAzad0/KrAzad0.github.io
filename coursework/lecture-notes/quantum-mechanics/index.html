Acknowledgments, references and academic integrity

These independent study notes are based on Quantum Mechanics–I coursework question sheets supplied by the Department of Physics & Nanotechnology, SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST), Kattankulathur. The original problems are acknowledged to the course context and instructors. The expanded explanations and solution manuscripts were compiled for study by Kumar Azad, with AI assistance in drafting and LaTeX typesetting.

The archive is not an official SRMIST answer key, has not been independently certified by instructors, and is neither an arXiv submission nor a peer-reviewed research paper. Mathematical statements should be cross-checked against the question sheets and reference textbooks before reuse or academic submission.

Useful background reading includes D. J. Griffiths and D. F. Schroeter, Introduction to Quantum Mechanics; J. J. Sakurai and J. Napolitano, Modern Quantum Mechanics; and R. Shankar, Principles of Quantum Mechanics. See each source manuscript for its detailed references and assumptions.

How to use these notes

Open a problem to read its complete online solution. MathJax renders LaTeX equations. The original .tex files preserve exact equation source, diagrams and detailed bibliography. On an assignment page, select “Expand all” then “Print / Save as PDF” to create a printable copy.
